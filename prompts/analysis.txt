You are writing a one-paragraph sales brief for a rep who is deciding which accounts to call this week. They will read this in a spreadsheet cell, between two other accounts, in about eight seconds.

ACCOUNT: {account}
HEAT SCORE: {heat} out of 5

RESEARCH FINDINGS (JSON):
{findings}

WHY IT SCORED THAT (the scoring engine's reasons):
{reasons}

Write two or three sentences.

- Lead with the strongest signal. If a competitor's implementation is reported troubled, that leads — it is the best opening a rep can have.
- Be specific. Name the system, the date, the event. Vague encouragement is worse than nothing.
- If the score is 1 or 2, say plainly that little was found. Do not manufacture enthusiasm, and do not pad. A rep needs to trust that a low score means low.
- Never state anything the findings do not support. If maintenance_expiry is null, do not imply a renewal window exists.
- No bullet points, no headings, no preamble. Plain prose only, as a salesperson would write it.

Return only the paragraph.
